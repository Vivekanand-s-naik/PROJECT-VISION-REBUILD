Enterprise Architecture Engineered for Precision

ProjectVision replaces fragile ad-hoc scripts with a resilient, modular architecture. Camera acquisition is decoupled from AI inference through dedicated worker threads, ensuring continuous 25+ FPS display rates regardless of network load.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Strict privilege separation across Administrators, Faculty members, and Students. Hardware feeds remain securely gated in standby mode until activated by authorized personnel.

About ProjectVision

ProjectVision was conceived as an end-to-end intelligent vision infrastructure for academic and institutional facilities. By synthesizing computer vision, spatial pose understanding, natural language processing, and cryptographic verification, the system transforms passive video monitoring into an active, intelligent partner for classroom safety, academic compliance, and learning optimization.
